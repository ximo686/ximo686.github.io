# Humanized Novel Prose

## Overview

This skill keeps the novel readable as fiction rather than as an explanation of fiction. The target style is detailed, smooth, psychologically grounded male-oriented web fiction with natural paragraph rhythm.

## When to Use

- Writing or rewriting chapter prose for this project
- Expanding chapter outlines into正文
- Fixing text that reads like AI, outline notes, or slogan lines
- Fixing text with too many short paragraphs, too many stock contrast sentences, or too much visible author arrangement

Do not use this to flatten all rhythm. Short sentences and short paragraphs are allowed when they create pressure, impact, or emotional interruption. They are not the default texture.

## Required Style Rules

1. **Thoughts should feel lived, not arranged**
   - Do not repeatedly externalize thinking through notebooks, lists, or explicit整理动作 unless the scene truly needs it.
   - Default to interior thought, observation, memory, bodily reaction, and associative judgment.

2. **Avoid stock AI sentence frames**
   - Strongly limit repeated use of:
     - `不是……而是……`
     - `不是……，而是……`
     - `不是……也不是……而是……`
     - `先……再……最后……`
     - `他知道……他更知道……`
     - `这不是……这是……`
   - Use them only when contrast is genuinely the strongest possible phrasing.
   - If a scene paragraph contains two contrast pivots of this kind, rewrite the whole paragraph instead of line-editing one sentence.
   - When revising chapters, treat this as a failure pattern, not as a style preference.

3. **Paragraphs must carry a complete beat**
   - Do not split into one sentence per paragraph by default.
   - A paragraph should usually hold one complete movement: action, emotional turn, memory fragment, or judgment chain.
   - One-line paragraphs are reserved for emphasis and should be rare.

4. **Sentences should open up**
   - Prefer developed sentences with texture, transition, and emotional carry.
   - Avoid stacks of overly short declarative lines that read like bullet points in disguise.
   - When a sentence only delivers conclusion, expand it until it also carries tone, pressure, sensory detail, or the character's immediate mental movement.
   - Transitional exposition should not sound like the author stepping out to summarize the point. Fold the explanation back into the current scene beat.

5. **Logic must be hidden inside prose**
   - The reader should understand why the character acts, but the explanation should emerge inside the flow of scene and thought.
   - Do not expose planning structure too directly.

6. **Character speech and inner conclusions must sound human**
   - Avoid slogan endings like `这次，我先走一步` unless the character would truly say it naturally in context.
   - Avoid empty posture lines whose main function is to sound cool.
   - Closing lines should come from the character's actual state of mind and immediate situation.
   - NPC dialogue must sound like a person in that world speaking from age, temperament, experience, and scene pressure, not like a quest description read aloud.
   - Avoid blunt task-briefing phrases that feel like system text wearing quotation marks, such as overly tidy instruction clusters or modern shorthand like `硬扫`, unless the speaker truly fits that register.
   - Dialogue should usually carry subtext: testing, impatience, caution, fatigue, distrust, habit, or old experience. Do not let every line speak in pure functional summary.
   - Older hunters, veterans, and hidden NPCs should speak with lived texture and implied history, not just clipped command lines.

7. **Chapter titles should feel like web-fiction chapter heads, not working notes**
   - Avoid over-plain titles such as `先把脚下踩实`, `第一桶金`, `冲十级` when a more scene-based or image-based title is available.
   - Prefer titles with object, place, danger, event, or strong image, for example: `裂谷回声`, `断崖藏匣`, `浅溪夺牌`.
   - Titles should be concise, but they cannot read like outline labels or spoken reminders.

## Rewrite Checklist

- Remove visible outline scaffolding from prose
- Merge broken one-line paragraphs into fuller scene paragraphs
- Replace repeated contrast templates with more natural transitions
- Check every `不是……而是……` structure and replace most of them with scene-led phrasing, reordered logic, or image-based expression
- Expand bare-bones sentences until they carry scene, tone, and motive
- Check the last 3 lines of the chapter for forced coolness or artificial punch

## Project-Specific Red Flags

- Repeated notebook or memo-taking used as a crutch for exposition
- Too many dramatic fragments in a row
- Reader can feel the author arranging logic instead of the character living through it
- Final line sounds like an AI quote card rather than a person finishing a thought
- The first 1-4 chapters circle the same emotional point without enough new information or action
- Early chapters rush straight to elite fights before showing normal combat adaptation and basic drop rhythm
- Other players are written as if they also carry future knowledge; they do not, they are first-time entrants still exploring
- Copy-related drops are exclusive to Lin Ye because only he has the SSS talent `复制`; normal players only see coins, gear, materials, consumables, quest items, and standard rewards
- System panels are presented too densely in one line; for readability they should usually use one stat per line or clear vertical grouping
- Combat skips enemy basics; ordinary and elite monsters should usually show at least name, level, HP, one or two skills, and the reason the fight is hard or easy
- Explanatory content must teach the reader the system through scene use: money, drops, quests, skills, panels, and talent rules should recur naturally rather than be assumed understood
- Dialogue that advances tasks or worldbuilding must still sound like speech, not like a design note wrapped in quotation marks
- If a paragraph's only job is to state the takeaway directly, expand or merge it until it also carries viewpoint, tone, or pressure
